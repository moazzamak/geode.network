## v6.1 amendment boundary

v6.1 preregisters two previously unopened capacity mechanisms: rank-32
tangent-space spherical caps and constrained nonnegative component weights. These
are new exploratory arms, not retunes or confirmation of the failed v6 branch.
A2 remains a development retention study; final-test labels stay sealed. If the
combined amended family remains more than 2.00 points behind the strongest
same-space control on S2, Outcome D becomes final for this predictive program.

The immutable A0 lock is stored in
`logs/results/v6_1/a0_parent_lock/`; the registered protocol is
`experiments/configs/v6_1/a0_parent_lock.json`.

The mechanisms are not claimed as method inventions. M29/M31 low-rank
components inherit PPCA/factor-analyzer and local-subspace precedents; M28 is
knowledge distillation/model compression; M30 belongs to directional statistics
and hyperspherical classification; A1-T uses principal-geodesic tangent-space
analysis; direct-label addition follows greedy RBF/matching-pursuit selection;
and A1-W follows two-stage RBF output-weight fitting. The precise attributions
and implementation differences are recorded in Section 1.1 of the v6.1 plan and
the attribution map and references [L68-L100] of
`analysis/RESEARCH_REPORT_v5.md`.

## A1-T result

The rank-32 tangent-cap arm stopped at seed-11 falsification. It reached 88.90%
development balanced accuracy and 0.3305 NLL, versus 89.50% and 0.2738 for the
frozen direct rank-32 affine control. The tangent arm therefore lost 0.60
percentage points and increased NLL by 20.69%; neither advancement path passed.
It used exactly 46 components, 584,476 stored scalar parameters, and 3,330
candidate evaluations. Exact fit/prediction replay, exact rollback, 100%
outside-region preservation, representation lineage, and sealed-test gates all
passed. Tangent caps do not advance to A2; A1-W proceeds on the affine rank-32
component set only.

## A1-W result

The constrained weighted affine readout passed seed-11 falsification. On the
identical frozen 46-component rank-32 set, it reached 91.60% development balanced
accuracy versus 89.00% for the exact equal-weight softmin parent, a 2.60-point
gain. NLL fell 13.44%. The model used 584,476 stored scalar parameters, including
all 46 weights, and its median inference-time ratio was 1.004, below the 1.2
ceiling. Exact fit/prediction replay, exact rollback, 100% outside-region
preservation, component immutability, and sealed-test gates passed.

Three of ten classes placed more than 90% weight on one component, below the
registered majority-collapse condition; this concentration remains a required
A2 report. The weighted affine arm advances to A2. Tangent weighting remains
closed because A1-T failed.

## A1-B result

The seed-11 component-budget diagnostic classified the direct-label rank-32
radial family as **unstable**, not budget-limited. Development balanced accuracy
rose from 72.00% at 10 components to a diagnostic maximum of 90.20% at 100,
then fell to 88.30% at 120. The registered material reversal from 100 to 120
components was -1.90 percentage points in accuracy with a 33.61% NLL increase.
The 80-to-120 endpoint slope was -0.35 points per ten components.

All eight registered counts (`10, 20, 30, 46, 60, 80, 100, 120`) used prefixes
of one deterministic 120-candidate bank and one nested greedy-selection
trajectory. The diagnostic performed 6,105 candidate evaluations at the full
bank, preserved representation lineage, and did not open final-test labels.
The 100-component result is not a new predictive arm or authorization to retune:
the A2 budget remains immutably fixed at 46 components.

## A2 result

The frozen three-seed weighted affine arm reached 91.73% mean development
balanced accuracy, 0.97 points above the 90.77% M31 parent. The strongest
same-space control remained RBF at 96.77%, leaving a 5.03-point gap. This
exceeds the registered 2.00-point parity operand, so the v6.1 predictive
amendment fails and **Outcome D is final**.

The weighted-minus-RBF seed-paired 95% interval was [-10.05, -0.01] points and
the pooled per-example bootstrap interval was [-5.98, -4.12] points. The
weighted-minus-M31 pooled interval was [+0.21, +1.72] points, while its
three-seed interval [-2.34, +4.27] included zero. Thus weighting improved the
retained explicit head but did not establish same-space parity.

Every seed passed byte-identical replay, exact rollback, 100% outside-region
preservation, the 46-component and 584,476-parameter limits, optimizer-work
limits, and the 1.2 inference-ratio ceiling. Weight concentration exceeded 90%
for 3, 4, and 4 of 10 classes on seeds 11, 23, and 37, respectively, but never
reached the registered majority-collapse condition. Final-test labels remained
sealed.

Per the final kill switch, tangent/readout predictive development stops. M32,
M33, predictive M35, M36, M37, topology, OOD, migration, amortization, and
confirmation remain closed as rescue paths. The independent Outcome-C
lifecycle frontier and physical E7 qualification remain eligible.

## A3 result

The independent Outcome-C frontier supports **rollback qualification only**.
The weighted affine and M31 heads each executed the four frozen edit operations
and exact rollback on seeds 11, 23, and 37. All 24 edit transactions restored
the canonical model and predictions exactly. The weighted head was
non-dominated among models with complete frontier points.

The broader lifecycle claim did not pass. Minimum unaffected-prediction
preservation was 84.2% for weighted affine and 86.2% for M31, below the frozen
99.9% locality contract. Prototype, RBF, and weighted kNN were accuracy-superior
to weighted affine, but lacked frozen comparable transactional update artifacts;
their missing costs were recorded as unsupported rather than zero. Therefore no
paired advantage over every accuracy-superior control was established.

The 5.03-point weighted-to-RBF predictive deficit remains prominent and Outcome
D is unchanged. A3 does not support a specialized lifecycle tradeoff,
comparative editability advantage, or predictive rescue claim.

## A4 result

The official Flowers five-shot tier, A4-F5, is feasible for rank 3. With exactly
five training examples per class, both rank-3 families satisfy `r+2=5`, fit one
primitive per class, and replay predictions and probabilities exactly. On the
official development split, rank-3 affine subspaces reached 88.24% balanced
accuracy and rank-3 tangent caps reached 90.20%. The declared controls reached
99.02% for linear logistic and 98.53% for both prototype and weighted kNN.

This is a support-feasibility result, not a competitiveness claim. Only the
official training partition was used for fitting; development was evaluation
only, and test features and labels were not loaded.

A4-F34 is **blocked**. The official training partition provides 5 labeled
examples per class, below the rank-32 requirement of `r+2=34`. Development and
test partitions were not combined with training. Neither Flowers tier changes
final DINOv2 Outcome D.
