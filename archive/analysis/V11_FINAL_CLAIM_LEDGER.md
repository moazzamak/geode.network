# GEODE v11 Final Claim Ledger

**Program:** Directional Conformal Support Envelopes over a Delegated Head
**Final outcome:** E
**Finalized:** 28 July 2026

## Registered question

V11 tested whether directional bounded tubes or atlases could act as calibrated
support envelopes over a frozen same-space discriminative head. The envelope
owned answerability, open-space safety, and lifecycle eligibility; the delegated
head owned class prediction. V11 did not reopen geometric classification
superiority or claim the true data manifold.

## Executed evidence

### M63: protocol, role, and conformal lock

M63 implemented spherical tangent geometry, split-conformal thresholds,
contrast acceptance, negative-guided extents, eight geodesic probe families,
separate envelope/head/composite endpoints, resource accounting, and exact
replay.

Before M65, a lineage audit found that the initially referenced M27 RBF artifact
had been trained on all ten CIFAR classes, including proxy-unknown classes 8 and
9. No real envelope had yet been fit. M63 was repaired to freeze a compliant
classes-0--7 RBF: fit only on `geometry_fit`, calibrated on
`score_calibration` without estimator retraining, and prediction-hash locked.
The corrected head reached 96.25% known development balanced accuracy, used
2,426 support vectors, and replayed exactly. M64 was re-locked to the corrected
M63 index and its conclusions were unchanged.

### M64: controlled directional identifiability and masking

Across seeds 11, 23, and 37, all nine straight S63 tubes recovered ranks 8, 16,
and 32 exactly. Pooled independent coverage was 92.389%; every 4x and 8x
tangent probe was rejected. Curved and two-mode supports benefited from two
patches, and full-cap, shell, random-orientation, and random-label controls
behaved as registered.

In the adversarial scene, the v10 normalized-minimum rule accepted 100% of 4x
masking probes. Contrast reduced acceptance to 0%. Negative-guided extents also
reduced acceptance to 0% at matched 92.462% coverage. This validates H1 only
under the registered controlled construction.

### M65: seed-11 real-feature screen

M65 split the frozen 1,600-observation calibration partition into disjoint
800-observation extent-fit and conformal subsets. All 27 registered rank by
patch-count by extent-policy cells failed before development-based selection:

- all 18 negative-guided cells were infeasible because another-class
  calibration observation remained inside at least one 0.90 own-class floor;
- all nine quantile cells exhausted margins 0, 0.05, 0.1, and 0.2 without a
  safety-feasible rule.

Quantile-cell system acceptance was 98.828--100% at 4x tangent extent,
92.578--98.828% at 8x, and 96.875--100% for 4x masking probes. Every
cross-class bridge was accepted. No cell was retained, and no predictive or
lifecycle claim was opened.

## Final interpretation

V11 has **Outcome E**: the directional conformal envelope line closes on these
frozen features. This is not the M64 kill-switch variant in which the controlled
masking mechanism is falsified; M64 passed. Instead, the registered mechanism
failed to transfer to real features:

1. contrast margins could not reject probes that competing components scored
   deeply inside calibrated support rather than as shallow near-ties;
2. negative-guided extents could not exclude other-class observations without
   crossing the registered 0.90 own-class floor; and
3. no real-feature cell reached evaluation eligibility, so H2, non-inferiority,
   three-seed confirmation, and lifecycle utility remain unsupported.

The durable result is narrow. Directional conformal tubes are identifiable and
safe in controlled scenes, but the registered explicit envelope does not
provide safe system-level support on the frozen DINOv2 features. This result
does not rule out nonlinear representations, alternative support estimators, or
other feature spaces.
